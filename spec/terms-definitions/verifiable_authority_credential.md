[[def: VAC, DTG authority credential, verifiable authority credential, DTG authority credentials, VACs]]

~ A type of [[ref: DTG credential]] that confers **authority**: it states what its subject may *do* at a named scope, as distinct from asserting something *about* the subject (an [[ref: VEC]]) or establishing that the subject *is* connected to a node (a [[ref: VMC]] or [[ref: VRC]]). A VAC names a scope — a [[ref: DTG node]] or a resource governed by one — and the actions the subject is permitted to take within it. A VAC may be **attenuated**: its holder may derive a further VAC conferring a subset of what they hold, bound to a narrower audience and a shorter validity period, without returning to the original issuer. Authority is not delegation: a VAC says the subject may act, in their own name, within the scope granted; it does not authorize acting *on behalf of* the issuer.
