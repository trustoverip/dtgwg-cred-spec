[[def: entity, entities]]

~ A party that can participate in [trust relationships](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:trust-relationship) in a [[ref: DTG]]. In the graph, an entity is represented by a [[ref: DTG node]]; see that term for the kinds of entity a DTG includes, such as persons, devices, AI agents, [[ref: VTCs]], [[ref: VTNs]], and services.
