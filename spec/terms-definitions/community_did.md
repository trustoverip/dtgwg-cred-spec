[[def: C-DID, community DID, C-DIDs]]

~ A [decentralized identifier](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:decentralized-identifier) (DID) used to identify a [[ref: VTC]] — or a [[ref: VTN]] — within a [[ref: DTG]]. The [[ref: VMC]] and [[ref: VIC]] schemas already issue to and from a VTN's C-DID, so the identifier covers both kinds of community node. (Superseded if [issue #22](https://github.com/trustoverip/dtgwg-cred-spec/issues/22) replaces the four VID types with a declared correlation scope, under which this identifier is simply `public`.)
