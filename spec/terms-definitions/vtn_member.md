[[def: VTN member, VTN members]]

~ A [[ref: VTC]] or another [[ref: VTN]] that has accepted a [[ref: VMC]] in a VTN by issuing the reverse VMC that completes the membership edge. In the VMC issued by the VTN, the member is identified using its own identifier as subject; the member issues its acknowledgement from that same identifier. Membership of a VTN is not transitive: the members of a member VTN are not thereby members of the VTN that lists it. A common pattern (depending on VTN governance) is for existing VTN members to invite new members by issuing them a [[ref: VTN invitation credential]].

~ VTN members are typically registered in one or more [trust registries](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:trust-registry) serving the VTN.
