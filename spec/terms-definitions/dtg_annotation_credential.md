[[def: DTG annotation credential, DTG annotation credentials]]

~ A type of [[ref: DTG credential]] that annotates an existing [[ref: DTG edge]] with additional assertions about the [trust relationship](https://glossary.trustoverip.org/#term:trust-relationship). The DTG Credentials Specification defines two subtypes of DTG annotation credentials: [[ref: VPCs]] and [[ref: VSCs]]; the [[ref: VEC]] and the [[ref: VWC]] are predicate profiles of the VSC.
